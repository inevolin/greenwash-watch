Outcome

user.define_outcome · max_iterations 1

Your definition of done: rubric.content (outcome.md v2, mode-neutral), graded by the platform every run.

  1. Does every assessed claim quote the brand's own words, with a fetched URL and date?
  2. Does every verdict (holds up / unsubstantiated / misleading) cite at least one independent source that contradicts or supports it, plus the relevant rule (UK CMA Green Claims Code, ASA CAP Code, EU Directive 2024/825 / UCPD, FTC Green Guides, or a national court ruling)?
  3. Is the wording defamation-safe: framed as "our assessment", with no allegations of intent or dishonesty beyond what a cited ruling found, and with "unsubstantiated" used where evidence is lacking?
  4. Was brand memory used: brands/.md read (if present) and updated with the last-checked date, verdicts and what changed? Rotation applies ONLY if the kickoff says "scheduled rotation run": then exactly the next brand in rotation.md was processed and the index advanced. On any other run, rotation.md must be untouched; do not penalise the absence of rotation.
  5. Is the EU Green Claims Directive (if mentioned) described accurately as a pending proposal, not law, and are there no fabricated rulings or URLs?
  6. Is every claim that a regulator or court has ruled on rated misleading or unsubstantiated, for a reason consistent with that ruling?
