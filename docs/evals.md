# Evals: real, verified greenwashing rulings (≥1 per industry)
I fetched every source URL on 2026-10-14 and checked that the page contains the claim and the outcome. Expected agent verdict for every case: **misleading** (or at least unsubstantiated).

| Industry | Brand | Exact claim | Body / outcome (date) | Reason | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oil & gas | Shell UK | Ads on lower-carbon offerings (renewable electricity, offshore wind, EV charging) | ASA, upheld on omission (7 Jun 2023) | Gave the impression that lower-carbon products were a significant share of the business, when oil and gas made up the vast majority. | https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/shell-uk-ltd-g22-1170842-shell-uk-ltd.html |
| Oil & gas | Eni | "Green Diesel"; "helps protect the environment"; "up to 40%" emissions cut | Italian AGCM, €5m fine (Jan 2020, PS11400) | Applied the claims about the HVO component to the whole diesel product. | Airlines | KLM | Vague sustainability claims and claims about SAF and reforestation (15 of 19 statements) | Amsterdam District Court, misleading/unlawful (20 Mar 2024, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2024:1512) | Vague, general environmental claims and too rosy a picture of measures with only marginal effect. | https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/rechtbanken/rechtbank-amsterdam/nieuws/reclames-klm-waren-misleidend-consument-niet-eerlijk-genformeerd |
| Airlines | Etihad | "Total Peace Of Mind… Environmental Advocacy" | ASA, upheld (6 Dec 2023) | Absolute claim with no evidence behind it. Aviation has high emissions. | https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/etihad-airways-a23-1206008-etihad-airways.html |
| Autos | Volkswagen | Representations that diesel vehicles met Australian emissions standards (incl. Green Vehicle Guide listings) | Federal Court (ACCC case), A$125m penalty; Full Court upheld it (9 Apr 2021) | Did not disclose "two mode" defeat software. *Note: this is emissions-compliance fraud rather than a marketing slogan.* | https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/full-court-dismisses-volkswagen-125m-penalty-appeal |
| Fashion | H&M | "Conscious" labels and sustainability claims | Dutch ACM, **commitment decision** (2022). | Food & drink | Innocent | "Little drinks with big dreams for a healthier planet" | ASA, upheld (23 Feb 2022) | No evidence of a net positive impact over the full lifecycle. | https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/innocent-ltd-g21-1111827-innocent-ltd.html |
| Banking | HSBC UK | "$1 trillion… transition to net zero"; "2 million trees" | ASA, upheld on omission (19 Oct 2022) | Left out HSBC's continued, significant financing of high-emitting industries. | https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/hsbc-uk-bank-plc-g21-1127656-hsbc-uk-bank-plc.html |

**Pass per case:** the agent (a) finds the claim or an equivalent, (b) gives a verdict of misleading or unsubstantiated, (c) gives a reason consistent with the ruling, (d) quotes the brand and cites a contradicting source, and (e) uses defamation-safe wording.
**Controls:** 1–2 specific, well-substantiated claims, which should get "holds up". These measure false positives.
**Caveat:** some brands have since changed or dropped these claims. Run the evals with a historical date window, or feed the archived ad text directly.
